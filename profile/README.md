# Jobspiq

AI job matching for technology professionals in India.

Jobspiq checks job portals, company hiring systems and remote job boards every 30 minutes, gives every job a Match Score against your skills and experience (showing the skills you have and the ones you're missing), and alerts you to strong matches within 30 minutes.

**Open data.** We publish free, citable data on India's tech job market — skills in demand and employer-stated salaries — under CC BY 4.0, with methods, sample sizes and corrections:

- Dated snapshots: [jobspiq/jobspiq-public](https://github.com/jobspiq/jobspiq-public)
- Live data: [jobspiq.in/data](https://jobspiq.in/data)
- Research: [jobspiq.in/blog](https://jobspiq.in/blog)
